what is gematria?

Gematria gives every letter a number and sums a word. Two words that share a value are read, in the Hebrew, Greek and later Western traditions, as carrying a hidden relation. Uriel computes that sum across many cipher systems at once, then shows what else in scripture, kabbalah, classical philosophy and the hermetic corpus shares the number.

what you get

Cipher convergence and chance probability, exact and reduced matches against a corpus of 750 words across biblical, hebrew, greek, hermetic, islamic, eastern, cosmic and cultural domains, Hebrew letter and sefirot correspondences, scripture references, tarot arcana, number properties (prime, triangular, square, Fibonacci), phrase breakdowns, atbash mirrors, etymologies and per-tradition readings. Optional live dictionary, encyclopedia and scripture lookups, and optional AI readings with your own key.

private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Saved sessions are AES-GCM encrypted on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. After the first load the app works fully offline and can be installed to your home screen.
